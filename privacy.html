Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Türkçe metin aşağıda.

This Privacy Policy describes how Semih Saydam ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game Multiplayer Battle (the "App").

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous player ID. To let you play online, the App creates an anonymous account through Unity Gaming Services. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Player name and in-game choices. The name you type in the menu, your class and team. Other players in the same room can see them.
  • Connection data. Your device's IP address is used to connect you to a game room, and connection latency is measured to pick the nearest server region. Precise location is never used.
  • Data stored on your device. Your settings (controls, sound, vibration, map theme), player name and class are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

What We Don't Do

The App shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, does not sell data and has no in-app purchases.

Third-Party Services

Online play uses services provided by Unity Technologies (Authentication and Multiplayer): Unity Privacy Policy. Test versions are distributed through Apple TestFlight; Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight with us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. To have your anonymous online account and its associated data deleted, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted at this URL.

Topladığımız bilgiler

  • Anonim oyuncu kimliği. Çevrimiçi oynayabilmen için uygulama, Unity Gaming Services üzerinden anonim bir hesap açar. Adın, e-postan ya da telefon numaran istenmez.
  • Oyuncu adı ve oyun içi seçimlerin. Menüde yazdığın ad, sınıfın ve takımın; aynı odadaki diğer oyuncular bunları görür.
