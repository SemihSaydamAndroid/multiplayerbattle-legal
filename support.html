Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play with friends?

Tap Çok oyunculu (Multiplayer), create a room and share the room code. Your friends enter the code and tap Odaya katıl. Empty slots are filled by bots.

I got disconnected during a match.

The game tries to reconnect automatically for a short while; a bot controls your hero until you're back.

The game is crashing / running slow.

Please make sure your iOS is up to date. If the issue persists, email us with your device model and iOS version.
